## 15. BUILD-SPECIFIC BETA EXPIRATION, TIME VERIFICATION, AND RESTRICTED-MODE ACCESS

15.1. **Fixed, build-specific expiration.** Each beta build contains a fixed expiration date and time determined by Licensor when the build is created (the "Beta Expiration Date"). That date and time are not calculated from, and are not reset by, installation, first launch, or acceptance of this Agreement. Licensee receives only the remaining time, if any, before the installed build expires. The Software displays the Beta Expiration Date in the device's local time in its About or Settings area and on its expiration screen. Display in local time does not change the underlying fixed expiration instant, regardless of time zone or device-clock changes. No minimum period of active beta access is guaranteed.

15.2. **Expiration and inability to establish trusted time.** The Software restricts active beta testing functionality when the Beta Expiration Date has passed. The Software also applies the same active-function restrictions if it cannot reliably establish the current time, including where its stored trusted-time record is unreadable or unusable. A time-verification restriction may be cleared by a successful online time check only when that check establishes that the installed build has not expired. If the installed build has expired, verification will not restore its active functionality.

15.3. **Categories of restricted functionality.** When the Software has expired or is operating under the time-verification restriction described in Section 15.2, Licensee may not start or continue live traffic capture, interception, forwarding, proxy or VPN transport, active network testing, or other network transmissions performed as part of testing. Attempts to send testing traffic are refused. Live browser operation, capture- and VPN-related configuration, certificate installation or capture setup, and controls for handling paused live traffic are unavailable to the extent restricted by the Software. Some nontransmitting testing or configuration screens may remain accessible, including editors, testing hubs, filters, project configuration, and informational views. Certain tools may also permit inspection or preparation of local drafts, including unsent requests. Access to a screen, editor, or locally stored draft does not authorize or enable transmission or continuation of active testing. These restrictions apply by functional category, including to comparable capabilities added in later builds, and do not imply that every screen associated with testing becomes inaccessible.

15.4. **Active sessions and paused traffic.** If a restriction under Section 15.2 takes effect while an active capture or network transport session is running, the Software is designed to stop the affected session gracefully. Traffic that is paused and awaiting a user decision at that time will be recorded as dropped rather than forwarded. Licensee acknowledges that such restrictions may interrupt ongoing tests, connections, or workflows and is responsible for planning testing activities accordingly.

15.5. **Continuing access to stored information and protective functions.** During restricted mode, Licensee may continue to view previously stored traffic, transaction details, analysis, findings, and other locally retained testing information, using the read-only features provided by the Software. Licensee may continue to use available data-removal functions, subject to lawful preservation duties. Those functions have limitations: removal from visible history may only mark encrypted database records as deleted without physically erasing them; full database reset is available only in certain database-unavailable states. The separate browser-data clearing control removes designated categories of browser data but may leave other website data, and does not remove traffic records from the encrypted capture database. The Software also remains available to display legal and privacy notices, expiration and clock-status information, and controls for user-initiated time verification. These permissions encompass later-added read-only review and protective data-deletion functions of the same kind, but do not authorize active testing or new traffic capture.

15.6. **Trusted-time record and clock-integrity protection.** To enforce the fixed Beta Expiration Date, the Software maintains a small encrypted record of the latest trusted time in application-private storage, using an encryption key held in the Android Keystore. The record is not intentionally transmitted over a network. The Software compares available time information against this record. If the device clock appears inconsistent, it may display a clock-suspect warning or banner and clamp suspect time values to prevent clock manipulation from extending active access. These protections do not alter the Beta Expiration Date. If the record is unreadable, unavailable, or otherwise unusable, the Software applies the active-function restrictions described in Section 15.2 until a successful online check establishes that the installed build has not expired. The Software is configured to disable Android cloud backup and exclude application data from Android device-to-device transfers; however, manufacturer-specific transfer tools may not honor these settings. Because the Android Keystore key is device-bound and is not transferred with application data, a copied encrypted time record is not expected to be readable on another device, which may require fresh time verification.

15.7. **Automatic and user-initiated online time verification.** When the Software cannot establish a reliable current date and the device has a network connection, it automatically attempts an online time check when the application is opened or returned to the foreground. On the screens shown when the build is expired or time cannot be established, Licensee may also initiate a check by selecting the available "Check again" control. A clock that is merely marked suspect does not by itself cause that control to be offered. Each attempt sends a minimal HTTPS HEAD request to a public website and reads date information from the response. The configured hosts are tried in order until an acceptable response is obtained; more than one host may be contacted if earlier attempts fail. The currently configured order is www.cloudflare.com, www.apple.com, www.microsoft.com, and www.google.com; it may change in later builds. The request is designed not to include cookies, account identifiers, device or installation identifiers, captured traffic, stored results, or other application-supplied personal identifiers. The receiving website can nevertheless observe the source IP address and ordinary connection metadata and may keep records according to its policies. A successful check only verifies the actual time. If an incorrect device clock made the build appear expired before its fixed Beta Expiration Date, correcting the clock and successfully selecting "Check again" may restore active functionality when the verified time confirms that the build remains valid. Verification cannot renew or extend a build whose fixed Beta Expiration Date has actually passed. See the Privacy Policy for additional information.

15.8. **No renewal or extension of an installed build.** The Beta Expiration Date embedded in a build cannot be changed or extended through an online time check, reinstallation of the same build, EULA acceptance, or resetting the device clock. If Licensor issues a newer build containing a later expiration date, Licensee may obtain continued active beta access by installing that newer build over the prior build in accordance with Licensor's instructions, subject to this Agreement and any updated terms requiring acceptance. Licensor makes no promise to release a newer build or extend beta availability. Licensee shall not circumvent expiration or clock-integrity controls except as permitted by applicable law.
